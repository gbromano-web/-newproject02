Last updated 21 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how RND Athletes ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit rndathletes.com (the "site").

Who we are

RND Athletes operates rndathletes.com. You can contact us about privacy at gbromano.5bdf@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use it only to reply.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, understand how it is used, and show advertising.

We may use Google AdSense to show ads on this site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before using cookies for personalised advertising, and you can change your choice at any time.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are popular. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information in aggregate form. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

Affiliate links

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The retailer may use cookies to record that you came from our site.

How we use information

We use information to run and improve the site, reply to messages, measure which content is useful, show advertising, and keep the site secure.

Legal basis (EEA and UK visitors)

We process personal data on the basis of your consent (for advertising and analytics cookies where required), our legitimate interests in running and improving the site, and, where relevant, to respond to your requests.

How long we keep information

We keep emails for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. Analytics and advertising data is kept according to the retention settings of those services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority. California residents may request information about the personal information collected and may opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. To make a request, email us at the address above.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

The site links to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

Changes to this policy
