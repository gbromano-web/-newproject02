Inside the RND Lab

Built in the lab.
Broken in on the road.

Every RND product starts with a problem runners tell us about. Our first collection is being developed around the four we hear most: weight, heat, sweat and chafing.

Lightweight knits

Weighs almost nothing.

Race tops and shorts built from light, breathable knits, so your kit feels the same at 40 km as it did at the start.

Status: in development

Sweat management

Dry before the next mile.

Fabrics chosen to move sweat away from the skin and dry quickly, for hot, humid training and long race days.

Status: in development

Chafe-aware fit

Moves the way you move.

Patterns that keep seams away from the places runners chafe most: under the arms, the inner thighs and the waistband.

Status: in development

Race-day carry

Fuel without the bounce.

Shorts and tops designed to carry gels securely at pace, so you can follow your fueling plan without a belt.

Status: in development

R&D is in our name.

RND started with a simple frustration: gear that looked fast but failed at kilometre thirty. While the collection is in development, the RND Journal shares the training know-how behind it: free plans, pace charts and race guides.
