We'd love to hear from you, whether it's a question about a training plan, a correction, or a partnership idea.

**Email:** [gbromano.5bdf@gmail.com](mailto:gbromano.5bdf@gmail.com)

We aim to reply within a few working days.

## What to contact us about

- **Training questions** about an article or plan in the Journal.
- **Corrections:** if you spot an error, tell us the page and what's wrong, and we'll fix it.
- **The shop:** questions about RND gear and the upcoming store launch.
- **Partnerships and press:** brands, race organisers, running clubs and media.

We can't give personal medical advice. If you're injured or unwell, please see a doctor or physiotherapist.
